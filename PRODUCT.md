## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind v4 + shadcn/ui (Base UI) + Motion, the standing stack for Whitesec sites (same as Smart Records Center). Local development first, folder `whitesec-energy-web`; deploy target and domain (whitesec.energy) not decided yet.

## Users

- **Energy buyers** who must decide whether, where and how to build or run an energy asset: oil and gas operators and KKKS procurement teams, IPPs and project developers, industrial companies building captive power, mine sites, palm oil mills, factories and large buildings, listed companies and large emitters, state-owned energy companies, investors and banks.
- They read English: Australian buyers and the Indonesian oil and gas and energy teams the owner targets. Each visitor asks three things: what is offered, does it fit me, how do I start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

whitesec.energy is the energy practice of PT Topi Putih Siberindo (Whitesec ID) and Whitesec AU: energy consulting and implementation in Indonesia and Australia for oil and gas, renewables and green energy. Seven services run in both countries and each client contracts with the local entity. Success: a visitor finds the service that fits, sees the local rules and the contracting entity for their country, and sends an enquiry with that service pre-selected.

## Positioning

One team that can run the feasibility study, set up the management system and secure the operating asset. The combination comes from Whitesec's existing work in audits, management systems and OT security. Positioning line: "Energy consulting and implementation for oil and gas, renewables and green energy in Indonesia and Australia."

## Operating Context

Structure (owner's plan docx, 24 October 2026): Home with seven menu items (Services, Indonesia, Australia, How we work, Experience, About, Contact); Services opens seven service pages built on one template. Services: Energy Project Studies; Records & Document Control; OT & Cyber Security for Energy; Energy Efficiency & ISO 50001; Decarbonisation & Emissions Reporting; Owner's Engineer & Technical Due Diligence; Renewable & Hybrid Solutions. Local rules and contracting entity per country come from the plan's "Delivery by country" table. Not offered at launch and never listed: hydrogen, carbon capture and storage, carbon credit development and trading, subsurface oil and gas work, grid connection studies in Australia.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: English only (owner instruction, 2026-10-27; an Indonesian version existed in an earlier build and was removed). Formal, professional tone.
- Oil and gas work for KKKS runs through CIVD-registered partners Manpro and SCU (Elnusa), as stated on whitesec.id. whitesec.energy does not claim its own CIVD registration (open item).
- Installation is built by licensed EPC partners; no partner is named until agreements are signed.
- ISO 50001 certification partners are not confirmed; the site does not name a certifier for it.
- Regulation citations come from the plan. Two items could not be verified and are kept off the public pages: Kepmen ESDM 96.K/DI.01/MEM.S/2026 and the 6,000 / 4,000 / 500 TOE thresholds. They are recorded in docs/OPEN-ITEMS.md.
- Release checklist (owner, 20 items): custom 404, unique titles and descriptions, CTA above the fold, favicon set, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, Open Graph image, alt text, mobile breakpoints, sticky mobile CTA, loading states, form error states, thank-you page, privacy policy, terms, cookie banner, analytics behind consent, real contact address, compressed images.

## Brand Commitments

Part of the Whitesec family: the orange cube mark, "energy" added to the wordmark, white background with orange only as an accent, one sans serif, real photographs of plants, solar arrays, control rooms and field sites (no bulb or leaf illustrations), one simple diagram per service page showing how the engagement runs. Tagline: the plan offers three options; this build uses "From feasibility study to a secure, efficient operation." (ID: "Dari studi kelayakan hingga operasi yang aman dan efisien."), to be confirmed by the owner.

## Evidence on Hand

Real: client logos and anonymised case studies on whitesec.id; certification and assurance partners on whitesec.id and whitesec.au (BSI, PECB, TUV SUD, SGS, Kiwa, RSM); founders and credentials on whitesec.au; office addresses, phone and email on both sites; the Perth address in the plan. No energy-project case study with client consent exists yet: the Experience page says so and shows only Whitesec ID work that is real. Photographs are free-licence images with recorded provenance (docs/IMAGE-CREDITS.md), shown as illustration, never as Whitesec's own projects. No statistics, testimonials, prices or certifications beyond the above may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Answer three questions on every page: what we do, whether it fits, how to start.
2. The local rule and the contracting entity are part of the offer, not fine print.
3. Evidence over claims: name only what is real, say what is not offered, omit what is not confirmed.
4. Formal clarity: plain, precise language a procurement officer and an engineer both trust.
